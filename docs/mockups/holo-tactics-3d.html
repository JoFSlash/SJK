Color rules
  • Gold is what is selected, and the one main action on screen (Fight).
  • Holo draws lines, outlines, Force (mana) and your half of the board.
  • Red belongs to the opponent: their half of the board, their shots and health.
  • Ember means loss or leaving: defeat, selling, the last seconds.
  • The Holo and Solar glow around a unit is a look from your cards. Cosmetic only.
